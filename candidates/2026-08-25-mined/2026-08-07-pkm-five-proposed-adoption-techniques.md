# Five evidence-backed PKM techniques proposed for adoption, strongest for the CIPPE ADHD user: spaced repetition, active recall, dual-coding, evergreen refinement, low-decision placement

## Body

Five techniques Anansi does not yet use, proposed for adoption: a human-facing spaced-repetition review scheduler; explicit active-recall self-testing over notes; a dual-coding visual or graph layer; evergreen refinement with versioned provenance; and predictable low-decision placement. These are proposals, not yet adopted decisions — held here as a candidate reference for a future adoption decision rather than ratified as a standing rule.
