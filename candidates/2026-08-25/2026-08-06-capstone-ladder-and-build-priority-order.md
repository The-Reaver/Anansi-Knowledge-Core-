## Capstone ladder (each rung adds one new hard skill; prove it and survive the Breakers before climbing; where possible the capstone is a real GEO Suite piece so training equals shipping)
- Rung 0. Notes app, one user. Save and load data (front end, back end, database).
- Rung 1. Notes app with accounts. Sign-up, login, password security (authentication and access control).
- Rung 2. Shared team board. Many users with roles and permissions (multi-user data and access rules).
- Rung 3. Live chat or shared document. Instant updates for everyone (real-time sync, the TikTok and Uber backbone).
- Rung 4. Paid plans or a simple store. Checkout and money that is never wrong (payments and transaction integrity, the fintech core).
- Rung 5. Analytics dashboard fed by a live source. Scheduled ingestion and background jobs (data pipelines).
- Rung 6. Ride or delivery matcher. Map, live location, match nearest (geolocation and matching, the Uber core).
- Rung 7. Social feed. Follows, likes, ranked feed (feeds, ranking, caching, the TikTok core).
- Rung 8. Survive load and crashes. Many users, failures, no data lost (scaling and resilience, where low-level and distributed-systems training pays off).
- Rung 9. Full capstone. One complete app combining real-time, multi-user, payments, location or feed, strict security, and scale, surviving the full Breakers gauntlet. The graduation project: build me an Uber, a TikTok, or a fintech app.
