# CIPP/E copilot, the intended user's profile, the Companion layer, and the tailored-intake principle (confidential)

## Body

## Confidentiality
- The intended user's identity is confidential. Never state her name. The software stores the name locally on her device; it is never spoken by the team or written into shared docs.

## The intended user (for build purposes, no name)
- A skilled CIPP/E privacy professional. Neurodivergent (ADD/ADHD). A skilled dancer (styles and disciplines to be learned by the app over time). Loves the book A Tale of Two Cities, among others.

## Design principles
- Tailored, not generic: the app learns her workflow and interests, keeps a private profile she controls, and continuously monitors how to serve her better.
- Meaningful, not cheesy: literary and understated touches tied to what she loves (for example themes of resilience, renewal, being "recalled to life" from A Tale of Two Cities), never corny quotes or emojis.
- Caring but correct: warm in tone, firm in substance. The copilot never just agrees; when a step would be non-compliant or wrong it says so plainly and cites the law. Her professional work done right is the goal, not flattery.
- Local and private: everything stays on her computer, one place. Cloud is optional, never required.

## Companion layer (prototype scaffold built on Lovable)
- A "You" personalization hub: private local profile, learned interests (editable), a "what I have learned about you" panel in calm correctable sections, and a gentle few-questions-at-a-time first run.
- A quiet "A moment for you" touch on the Now dashboard, meaningful and dismissible, tied to what she loves.
- One gentle adaptive suggestion based on a noticed workflow pattern, acceptable or dismissible.

## Tailored intake and continuous improvement
- What the app learns about her is stored in compartmentalized rooms (how she works, focus patterns, interests), correctable by her, and continuously improved. Mirrors the Knowledge Core room structure.

## Pending (deep research + Gemini/Quinn meta-analysis, redesign phase)
- The set of intuitive professional questions the agent should ask a CIPP/E professional.
- The fully local, private setup (single-container local build on her machine).
- Significant neurodivergent UX research and the Notion-style nav drawer.
