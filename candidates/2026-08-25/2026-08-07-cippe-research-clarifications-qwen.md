# CIPP/E research clarifications (answers to Qwen), executive-function priority, personalization model, interview triggers

## Body

## Executive-function priority (ranked)
1. Working-memory offloading (core, the Externalized Thread: app holds plan, context, clock).
2. Task initiation (one clear "right now" step; resume after interruption).
3. Reduce decision fatigue (strong defaults, one primary action, few choices).
4. Time management (ambient, gentle, never time pressure).
- Emphasis: structured defaults and scaffolded workflows first; reminders calm and secondary.

## Adaptive personalization model
- Hybrid, explicit-led. Implicit repeated behavior forms a hypothesis; act only after explicit confirmation (suggest, she accepts). Never silently change; stable layout across sessions.
- Transparency lightweight: one-line "because" per suggestion, plus a visible, editable "what I've learned about you" memory she controls. Not a report.

## In-app interview triggers
- Priority: user-initiated (always reachable, in her control).
- Then gentle milestone-triggered questions at natural pauses (e.g., after a completed task), one small question, dismissible, never mid-focus.
- Calendar intervals only as a soft, infrequent, dismissible nudge, never a scheduled demand.
- Responses feed BOTH interface customization (show/hide, ordering, focus windows) AND content suggestions. Every interview tiny and low-pressure.
