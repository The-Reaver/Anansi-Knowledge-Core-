# A dedicated neurodivergent-experience specialist agent, and the operator-plus-Claude meta-analysis research method

## Body

## The specialist agent
- Designate an agent whose entire remit is neurodivergent experience design: the Externalized Thread, the Notion-style navigation drawer, the adaptive feedback loop, COGA + WCAG 2.2 AA, and a reusable neurodivergent acceptance checklist every product passes before shipping.
- Precedent: Orlok already owns the accessibility and neurodivergent surface (per the CIPP/E ledger). Open decision for the operator: Orlok specialized, or a new agent alongside Orlok.
- Operator names the agent (standing rule: the operator names every agent before it is born). PENDING operator input: the name, and Orlok-vs-new.

## The meta-analysis research method (repeatable)
- Operator gathers from Gemini and Quinn. Claude gathers from its sources. Claude then runs a meta-analysis: merge both, dedupe, weight each finding by evidence quality, flag disagreements, adversarially verify shaky claims before any finding becomes a design rule.
- This is a reusable collaboration pattern: the operator can add research depth anytime, and Claude folds it into a weighted synthesis.

## What the operator's Gemini/Quinn runs should target
- Evidence-based ADHD and neurodivergent UX patterns; executive-function support in software; calm, non-nagging reminder and notification design; progressive disclosure and low-cognitive-load navigation; COGA cognitive-accessibility techniques; concrete examples of tools neurodivergent people love and why (Notion, Todoist, others). Academic and clinical sources weighted heavily.

## Timing
- Runs in the post-v1 redesign phase, after all CIPP/E features exist, so the neurodivergent layer is designed against the finished app.
