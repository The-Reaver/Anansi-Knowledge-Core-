# PKM meta-analysis reconciled: hub and weak-claims watchlist

## Body

An external evidence-based meta-analysis of seven personal-knowledge-management methods was reconciled against our formulas research and the Anansi and CIPPE design. It validates Anansi's core: atomic single-idea notes, reformulation in your own words, typed rather than bare links, and provenance-as-metacognition are all backed by cognitive science, and the Zettelkasten model scores highest on depth of encoding, association fidelity, creativity, and metacognition.

Five evidence-backed techniques we do not yet use are proposed for adoption, strongest for the CIPPE ADHD user: a human-facing spaced-repetition review scheduler, explicit active-recall self-testing over notes, a dual-coding visual or graph layer, evergreen refinement with versioned provenance, and predictable low-decision placement.

Governance flag: the document's headline "effectiveness formula" is a hand-assigned, unvalidated weighted-sum heuristic, so under our formula-first rule it may be used only as a framing heuristic, never cited as an evidence-based formula.

Weak-claims watchlist, do not repeat as fact: the "mind maps improve recall 32 percent" figure is one study of children on word recall; mind mapping g equals 1.54 is a subgroup value not the headline g equals 0.73; the concept-mapping 0.776 effect is labeled both Cohen's d and Hedges g; Luhmann's book output is survivorship bias, not proof of causation; PARA, Johnny.Decimal, Evergreen, and outliner systems have testimonial or inferential support only; and left-brain/right-brain is a neuromyth, the real mechanism is dual-coding. Full detail and the 19 candidate notes are in the reconciled artifact held for approval. A second research document is expected and will be reconciled against this one.
