# The SiteGen design system is the most complete of the six areas — atomic, ratification-gated, and already producing real artifacts

## Body

Audited 2026-08-31. `SPEC_SITEGEN_DESIGN_SYSTEM` defines an atomic system with a strict
build order: **tokens → components → sections → page templates → themes**. Nothing enters
the system without ratification **and** a Jasiah discrimination harness — the design
equivalent of a proof gate, so an addition has to be shown to be distinguishable rather
than merely asserted to be new.

It has shipped real output, not just specification. Sprint C3 produced an actual
`tokens.css`. Sprint C4 added a prototype and a screen-to-data map. The CIPP/E direction
ruling and the HBOT theme were both walked through the fidelity ladder.

Of the six dormant areas this is the one whose mechanism demonstrably **runs**: work enters,
gets gated, and comes out the other side. It is the closest thing the fleet has to a working
model of the loop every other area is missing, which makes it worth studying as a template
rather than only cultivating as an asset.

The qualification: its declared upstream feed — Oluwole's research folding into the
principles — has never executed once. So the system is well-built and running on a starved
input.
