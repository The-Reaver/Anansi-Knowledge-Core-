# A hunting team turns the Breakers from a test into an exercise both sides learn from

## Body

Operator ruling, 2026-09-01: alongside the attacker agents there should be a **hunting team** —
which can simply be the existing development team with added skills — whose job is to **hunt down
the attackers**. The two sides meet periodically, war-game style, and the fleet learns from the
encounter.

This is the missing half of the Breakers design. As ratified, the gauntlet is one-directional: the
Breakers attack, the build passes if they come up empty. That measures whether an attack succeeds.
It does **not** measure whether an attack would be **noticed** — and in a system holding patient
data under a never-leaves constraint, undetected intrusion is the failure that matters most. A
breach nobody sees is indistinguishable from no breach, which is the same false-green shape this
Core has recorded repeatedly.

Making the defenders the **development team with added skills**, rather than a separate unit, is
deliberate and correct: the people who build the system learn its weaknesses by defending it, and
the skill stays where the code is written.

**Two measures fall out of this**, and both are new: **time to detect** and **time to respond**.
Neither exists today, because nothing currently hunts. They belong in the war-game record beside
the break log the Breakers already owe.
