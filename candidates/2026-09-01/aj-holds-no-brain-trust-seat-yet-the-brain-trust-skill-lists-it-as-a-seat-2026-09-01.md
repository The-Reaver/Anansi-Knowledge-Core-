# Two ratified artifacts disagree about whether AJ sits on the Brain Trust, and the skill is the one that is wrong

## Body

`AJ/AJ_HARDWIRING.md` — ratified by the operator 2026-08-01, and marked **"locked; do not weaken
without a new operator ratification"** — states AJ's isolation mechanics plainly:

> Holds no Brain Trust seat, no vote, no build or Orlok role, **ever**.
> ... Audits everyone, including Amadeus and the rest of the Brain Trust.

`skills/stag-brain-trust/SKILL.md` lists the eight seats as: *"Elijah, Orlok, Celestina, TYR,
PrivacyDomain, Amadeus, **AJ**, Oluwole."*

**These cannot both be true.** The skill grants AJ a seat and a vote that the charter forbids
forever. And the seat is not theoretical: the 2026-08-08 Curiosity/Solutions Room proposal records
its ratification as *"unanimous by Celestina, Oluwole, Amaya, Jasiah, Omar, Sentinel, AJ"* — AJ
voting on a governance artifact it would later be expected to audit.

The charter wins. The operator confirmed the intent directly on 2026-09-01: **AJ is an auditor, it
sits outside, and it does independent analysis.** An auditor holding a vote on the body it audits
destroys the independence the whole role exists to provide — the same structural defect as a
security auditor grading its own security work, which this Core already ruled against in
`2026-08-06-tyr-never-certifies-itself-aj-plus-second-breaker-do`.

**TYR carries the same defect.** TYR is approved as lead Breaker "independent of the fleet,
reporting to AJ", yet also appears as a Brain Trust seat in the same skill. Both need removing.

**Action:** strike AJ and TYR from the Brain Trust seat list, take the roster to six, and record
whether any past verdict turned on AJ's or TYR's vote.
