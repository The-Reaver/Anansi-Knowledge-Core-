# AJ must be a master of development and of design, because an auditor cannot audit what it does not understand

## Body

Operator ruling, 2026-09-01: AJ has to be **a master at development and a master at design**, and
must be fed the same knowledge and abilities that a human conducting an audit would need to have
about the thing they are auditing.

This closes a gap in AJ's existing charter. `AJ_HARDWIRING.md` specifies AJ's **independence** in
detail — no seat, no vote, no ledger rating, artifacts only, one sealed output — but says nothing
about its **competence**. Independence without expertise produces an auditor that can only check
whether documents exist, not whether the architecture they describe is sound. That is process
theatre, and it is precisely the failure this fleet keeps recording: a control that runs, reports
clean, and was never capable of catching the thing it was pointed at.

The operator's framing is the right test: a real audit firm sends people who know the domain better
than the team being audited. AJ should be held to that bar.

**What follows concretely:** AJ needs its own curriculum — development and design mastery — on the
STARS/DREAMS pattern the fleet already uses for other agents, and it must be re-levelled as the
stack it audits changes. An auditor whose knowledge is a snapshot audits a system that has moved.
