# Chat triage filter: "all types of chats are eligible" does not mean every chat gets a note

## Body

Adopted as the standing rule (Bink's triage filter): no category of chat — coding, strategy, Lovable-build, personal reminders — is excluded from eligibility up front. But "all types" does not mean every chat regardless of substance gets a note. A chat that produced no real decision, commitment, correction, or reusable outcome gets skipped entirely, not even a stub note. This keeps Knowledge Core's signal-to-noise usable.
