# The First STAG Readiness Ranking Wrongly Treated "CIPP/E Agent" as Compliance Intelligence

## Body

When Abad first asked for the STAG projects ranked by readiness with emphasis on getting "the CIPP/E agent" ready after GEO, the first version of that ranking treated "CIPP/E agent" as the Compliance Intelligence platform. That was wrong. Abad meant a separate, purpose-built project: the TONY, LLC CIPP/E Privacy Agent, built for a neurodivergent CIPP/E-certified professional and handed to Antigravity to build. The mistake stayed in the artifact until Abad corrected it directly. The ranking was rebuilt to separate the two projects correctly.
