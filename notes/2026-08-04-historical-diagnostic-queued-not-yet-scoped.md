# A Full Historical Diagnostic Into Knowledge Core Was Queued, the Brain Trust Did Not Approve It As Scoped

## Body

Abad asked to queue a multi-session effort working directly with this assistant to capture everything ever decided across all past STAG chats into Knowledge Core as atomic notes, describing it as ongoing and possibly running past this Friday, with instruction to get more aggressive about closing out all chats and writing full atomic notes afterward, covering every type of chat. The Brain Trust reviewed this alongside Mandate 10 and did not approve it as scoped. Amadeus and Elijah want it sequenced after the Anansi minimal slice ships and piloted narrowly rather than run unbounded, since it currently competes with GEO and CI, the standing top priority. Celestina, Oluwole, and Jasiah want a dedup-and-quality gate over a volume-or-deadline gate, warning that "more aggressive after Friday" risks reproducing the exact low-substance, transcript-not-artifact problem Mandate 9 was written to prevent. Bink wants a triage filter so only chats that actually reached a decision get a full atomic note, rather than diluting Knowledge Core with low-signal sessions. This is not yet resolved with Abad; it stays queued, not scoped or greenlit as described.
