# Mandate 9: A Task Only Counts as a Compounding Asset If It's Captured, Reused, and Measured

## Body

Abad proposed a new standing mandate: only invest in agent-capability work that compounds, and the STAG Brain Trust (simulated in this Cowork session, not the real fleet) ratified it 6-0 with amendment. Final text: a rep, validation pass, or curriculum task only counts as building a compounding asset when it is captured in a reusable artifact rather than a session transcript, that artifact is later consumed by a different agent or a later session without re-deriving the lesson, and that consumption produces a measurable outcome delta, not a narrative claim of improvement. Task volume alone, however large, does not satisfy this mandate. Oluwole's adopted caution: a low certification score is not automatic proof this mandate is being violated, check whether the real cause is a design gap or missing infrastructure first.
