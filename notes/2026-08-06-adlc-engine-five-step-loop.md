# The ADLC Engine: Assign, Build, Prove, Capture, Level up — repeating on every task

## Body

The Agent Development Life Cycle's Engine runs on every single task and repeats forever: (1) Assign — the curriculum (STARS) hands the agent its next task; (2) Build — the agent builds it as one small slice; (3) Prove — it must pass verify.py green or a proving artifact, no proof no credit (Mandate 7); (4) Capture — the lesson becomes an atomic note in the Knowledge Core (Mandate 8); (5) Level up — the tracker (DREAMS) records the win and raises the score. Then back to Assign for the next task.
