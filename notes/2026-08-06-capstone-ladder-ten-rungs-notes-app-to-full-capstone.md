# The capstone ladder: ten rungs from a single-user notes app to the full combined capstone surviving the Breakers gauntlet

## Body

Each rung adds one new hard skill; it must be proven and survive the Breakers before climbing. Where possible the capstone is a real GEO Suite piece, so training equals shipping. Rung 0: notes app, one user (save/load, front end, back end, database). Rung 1: notes app with accounts (sign-up, login, password security). Rung 2: shared team board, many users with roles and permissions. Rung 3: live chat or shared document, instant updates for everyone (real-time sync, the TikTok/Uber backbone). Rung 4: paid plans or a simple store (checkout and money that is never wrong, the fintech core). Rung 5: analytics dashboard fed by a live source (scheduled ingestion, background jobs). Rung 6: ride or delivery matcher (map, live location, match nearest — the Uber core). Rung 7: social feed (follows, likes, ranked feed — the TikTok core). Rung 8: survive load and crashes (many users, failures, no data lost). Rung 9: full capstone — one complete app combining real-time, multi-user, payments, location/feed, strict security, and scale, surviving the full Breakers gauntlet — build me an Uber, a TikTok, or a fintech app.
