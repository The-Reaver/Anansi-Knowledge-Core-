# GEO Suite product definition: one platform, three tools sharing one memory and rulebook — Visibility, Compliance Intelligence, Sales

## Body

GEO Suite is one platform for medical practices, built as three tools under one roof: Visibility (get found by patients and AI search), Compliance Intelligence (catch legal problems, attorney-reviewed), and Sales (a ranked call list with evidence and talking points). All three share one memory (the Knowledge Core) and one rulebook. The Compliance Intelligence tool is a standalone product under the umbrella, extractable later as its own offering.
