# GEO Suite two-day demo locked scope: one real medical practice through a seven-step click-through in about five minutes

## Body

Goal: show one real medical practice go through GEO Suite from start to finish — found, audited, reported, improved, handed to sales — so it feels like a real product, not a plan. The click-through: (1) Dashboard, showing three pillars (Prospecting, Compliance and Reports, Sales Pipeline); (2) Prospecting, a ranked call list of real California practices from real lead data, an HBOT one highlighted; (3) Audit, the seven-category AI-Search Readiness score plus compliance findings, each with a citation, real engine output; (4) Report, findings grouped by risk each with a citation and score, the prospect-facing report; (5) The fix, an improved AI-ready site preview scoring above the 90 gate, before and after; (6) Sales kit, issues/citations/score/talking points for the practice; (7) Pipeline, the practice moving discovered to called to closed. Day one: pick the example HBOT practice, run the real audit, wire dashboard/prospecting/audit/report to it. Day two: before/after site, sales kit and pipeline, stitch into one smooth clickable flow, polish, rehearse the five-minute walk-through.
