# For a real production system holding patient data and money, keep at least one experienced human engineer for security review and accountability

## Body

A strong GEO Suite demo in two days is achievable with Claude building alone — that is the right two-day target, a demo, not a hardened production system. For a live, high-stakes system handling patient data and money, keep at least one experienced human engineer for security review and accountability. Not because Claude cannot build it, but because a live high-stakes system needs a responsible human. The honest picture is far fewer people plus a human for security and accountability, not nobody.
