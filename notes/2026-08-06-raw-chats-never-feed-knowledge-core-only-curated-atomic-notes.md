# Do not dump raw chats into Knowledge Core, including unrelated ones — distill each chat into curated, cited, verified atomic notes; keep unrelated personal material separate

## Body

Raw dumps would poison the Core with noise and wrong turns. Capture everything worth keeping, but as clean notes, not raw transcripts. Keep versatility by tagging and segmenting by topic, and keep unrelated personal material in its own space, not mixed with build knowledge.
