# Rule: every chat title carries a date and timestamp

## Body

From 2026-08-07 onward, every conversation title includes a date and a timestamp. Rationale: harvested atomic notes need precise provenance, and dated titles let chats be ordered, found, and matched to the notes drawn from them. This closes a real weakness seen in the legacy chats, which had no reliable date handle and drifted out of sync unnoticed. Applies to all future sessions across the fleet.
