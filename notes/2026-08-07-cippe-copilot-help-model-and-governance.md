# CIPP/E copilot model: inline contextual suggestions (no separate chat window), local RAG over GDPR/CPRA/CPPA/EDPB sources, decision-support ranked above automation ranked above paste-and-import integration

## Body

Inline contextual assistance: highlight a field, get an inline suggestion, substitute only on approval — avoids the context switch of a separate chat window. Local model with semantic retrieval (RAG) over GDPR, CPRA, CPPA ADMT rules, EDPB guidance, and local policies; cited answers, drafting/redaction, risk categorization (Low/Med/High). Feature ranking: decision support > automation (templates, deadline clocks, auto-populate from import) > constrained paste-and-import integration (no live external APIs). Governance model: the solo operator acts as centralized authority over the tool's content, while the tool itself can still output developer-friendly ("spoke") guidance to others.
