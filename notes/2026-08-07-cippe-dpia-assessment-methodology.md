# CIPP/E DPIA methodology: CNIL process, EDPB nine-criteria threshold screening, guided risk matrices, jurisdiction-specific templates (not one generic PIA)

## Body

CNIL methodology; EDPB nine high-risk criteria threshold screening (advise the user a full DPIA is likely not required when fewer than 2 criteria trigger). Guided risk matrices with pre-built threat vectors, not a blank table; auto-calculate residual risk after mitigations. Jurisdiction-specific templates: GDPR DPIA vs CPRA Risk Assessment vs AI/Algorithmic Impact Assessment — do not homogenize into one form; this was a resolved conflict between the research sources.
