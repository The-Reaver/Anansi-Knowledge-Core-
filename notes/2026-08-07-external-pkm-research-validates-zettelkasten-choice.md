# An external evidence-based meta-analysis of seven PKM methods validates Anansi's Zettelkasten design as highest-scoring on depth of encoding, association fidelity, creativity, and metacognition

## Body

Reconciled against our formulas research and the Anansi/CIPPE design: atomic single-idea notes, reformulation in your own words, typed rather than bare links, and provenance-as-metacognition are all backed by cognitive science. Of seven personal-knowledge-management methods evaluated, the Zettelkasten model scores highest on depth of encoding, association fidelity, creativity, and metacognition — independent validation of the approach Anansi already uses.
