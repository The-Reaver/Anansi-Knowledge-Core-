# PKM research weak-claims watchlist: the mind-mapping 32%/g=1.54 figures, the concept-mapping 0.776 label, Luhmann survivorship bias, and the left/right-brain neuromyth must not be repeated as fact

## Body

The "mind maps improve recall 32 percent" figure is one study of children on word recall; mind-mapping g=1.54 is a subgroup value, not the headline g=0.73. The concept-mapping 0.776 effect is labeled both Cohen's d and Hedges g inconsistently across sources. Luhmann's large book output is survivorship bias, not proof of causation for the Zettelkasten method. PARA, Johnny.Decimal, Evergreen, and outliner systems have only testimonial or inferential support, not controlled evidence. Left-brain/right-brain is a neuromyth; the real underlying mechanism is dual-coding.
