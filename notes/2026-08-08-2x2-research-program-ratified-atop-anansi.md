# Operator ratified the 2x2 troubleshooting-narrative research program; it now sits at the top of Anansi and is meant to feed everything else

## Body

Abad ratified the findings and recommendations from the completed 2x2 research program (Batch
9/Topic 16, Batch 11/Topic 18, Batch 12/Topic 19, see
`2026-08-08-2x2-troubleshooting-narrative-program-complete.md`) and directed that it becomes the
top-priority organizing input for Anansi going forward, feeding everything else the Knowledge Core
does.

One honest caveat carried over from that program, unchanged by ratification: none of the three
legs has been run through Qwen or Gemini for the three-way reconciliation this research system was
designed for, so the underlying findings remain single-source. Ratifying the recommendation to
prioritize this direction is a separate act from ratifying every individual factual claim inside
the briefs; the claims still carry their own confidence and confirmed/believed-unconfirmed labels
and should be read that way even after this ratification.

What "feeds everything" should concretely mean is not yet specified beyond the ratification
itself: whether the solution-artifact packaging schema becomes a required field on new curriculum
entries, whether the intake pipeline stage-mapping becomes the default vetting process for all
future research batches, or some narrower scope. Recording that clarification is next-session work,
not yet done.
