# An unresolved contradiction between a candidate note and an already-ratified Core note always routes to the operator for an explicit decision, regardless of the candidate's other rubric scores

## Body

Under the anansi-promote skill's rubric, a candidate note that unresolvedly contradicts an already-ratified note in the permanent Knowledge Core must always be routed to the operator for an explicit decision, regardless of how high the note scores on the other four rubric dimensions. The reviewing process is never supposed to auto-resolve which of two conflicting claims is correct by picking one side; that judgment call belongs to the operator alone.
