# The anansi-promote skill scores every candidate note on five dimensions, novelty, evidence strength, actionability, generality, and non-contradiction, and combines the total with a stress test into one of four promotion bands

## Body

The anansi-promote skill's real promotion rubric scores each candidate note on five dimensions, each worth 0, 1, or 2 points: novelty (does the Core already know this), evidence strength (speculative versus observed-once versus observed-repeatedly-or-root-caused), actionability (can a different agent act on it without re-deriving it), generality (one incident versus a reusable pattern), and non-contradiction (does it conflict with something already ratified). The five scores sum to a total out of ten, which combines with a required stress test to produce one of four bands: 8 to 10 with a surviving stress test promotes the note as-is; 5 to 7 promotes it with a stated revision; an unresolved contradiction with an existing ratified note always escalates to the operator regardless of the other scores; and below 5, or a failed stress test even at a higher score, rejects the note with a stated reason.
