# A candidate note must survive a deliberate stress test, a concrete counterexample where its lesson would give the wrong answer, before it can be promoted as-is under the anansi-promote rubric

## Body

Before finalizing a promote-as-is verdict under the anansi-promote skill's rubric, the reviewer must actively try to break the candidate note's claim: construct a concrete counterexample where following the stated lesson would give the wrong answer, and check whether the claim is really a general principle or just one incident dressed up as one. A candidate that scores high on the five-dimension rubric but has not been put through this stress test is not the same as one that was tried and held; a high score with no stress test drops a band even though the numeric total looks the same.
