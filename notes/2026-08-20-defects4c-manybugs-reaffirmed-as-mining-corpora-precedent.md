# The Defects4C, ManyBugs, and Defects4J research corpora were reconfirmed as a real, working precedent for sourcing citable memory-safety bug reproductions, verified against on-disk artifacts

## Body

During this session's review of a ten-file series of mined memory-safety bug reproductions dated 2026-08-09, the underlying research corpora used to source those reproductions, Defects4C and the related ManyBugs and Defects4J collections, were reconfirmed as a real, working precedent for sourcing citable, reproducible bug-fix examples, with every individual mined bug's on-disk reproduction artifacts independently verified to actually exist as claimed rather than being taken on faith.
