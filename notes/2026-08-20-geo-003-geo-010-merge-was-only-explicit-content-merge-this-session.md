# Across an entire session's candidate-backlog review, exactly one pair of duplicate candidates was resolved by an explicit content merge rather than a straightforward reject-in-favor-of-the-other

## Body

Across the entire candidate backlog reviewed this session, one specific pair of duplicate candidates, both describing the same finding that a GEO platform database defect was really two incompatible schema drafts colliding rather than merely duplicate filenames, was the only case handled by an explicit content merge: the more specific of the two notes was rejected as a standalone entry, but its distinguishing table-level technical detail was folded directly into the body of the more general note before that one was promoted. Every other duplicate found elsewhere in the session was resolved by rejecting one copy outright in favor of the other, without a content merge.
