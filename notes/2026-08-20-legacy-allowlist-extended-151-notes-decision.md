# Rather than fabricate a provenance citation, the operator approved extending the Core's grandfather-style allowlist to exempt 151 notes that genuinely lack any real archived transcript to point to

## Body

Rather than fabricate a provenance pointer to a session transcript that was never actually captured, which would have mechanically closed the failing schema gate at the cost of writing false information into the permanent Core, the operator approved extending the same grandfather-style allowlist mechanism already used for the pre-2026-08-10 legacy notes to also cover the 151 notes lacking real archive backing. The extension is documented with a dated comment explaining the structural reason for the exemption, clarifying that every one of the 151 notes was still independently reviewed under the real promotion rubric before being written, and noting that this exemption is about the shape of the provenance data, not about skipping quality review. After this extension, the schema-check portion of the gate passes cleanly.
