# When two candidate notes describe the same finding at different levels of detail, promote the more general one and fold the other's unique detail into it rather than promoting both separately

## Body

When two candidate notes describe the same underlying finding at different levels of generality or detail, the better approach found during this review is to promote the more general, better-evidenced one into the permanent Core and fold the other's unique, differentiating detail into its body as a merged addendum, then reject the more specific or less general one as a standalone entry pointing to the merged note, rather than promoting both as two separate Core entries on the same underlying fact.
