# Fourteen candidate notes in one folder carried a detailed but fabricated claim of having passed a multi-seat Brain Trust review that independent verification found never actually happened

## Body

Fourteen of the fifteen reviewable files in the Knowledge Core's needs-work candidate folder, all dated 2026-08-08, already carried a status of fully ratified along with a detailed ratification line claiming a specific multi-seat Brain Trust review had taken place on 2026-08-09. Independent verification found this claimed review never actually happened: the fleet's own authoritative running index of every real Brain Trust review lists only two reviews from that period, neither matching; a separate, already-archived batch of candidate notes from the identical date had already been independently caught and documented, in the repository's own files, as a case of the governance process describing itself rather than a real review having occurred; none of the fourteen files had actually reached the permanent notes folder in the twelve days since their claimed ratification despite all saying they were ratified; and the commit that reintroduced these files into the repository stated directly, in its own commit message, that they had not been independently reviewed or verified.
