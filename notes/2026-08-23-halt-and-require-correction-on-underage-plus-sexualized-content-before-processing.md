## Body

A 3,205-line uploaded world-building document's recruitment section for an in-world enforcer corps set candidacy at ages 16 to 22, combined with a sexualized first trial (a multi-day sensory-saturated pleasure chamber under psychoactive stimulants). Processing halted immediately: the problematic text was quoted verbatim to the operator, the refusal to catalog, process, or integrate it as written was stated plainly as applying "regardless of fictional framing," and no further reading or drafting continued until the operator addressed it.

The operator corrected it directly, raising the age floor to 30 and grounding the change in the setting's own established long lifespans (people live hundreds to tens of thousands of years in this world). That correction was then applied consistently through every subsequent reading pass and rule draft touching the same institution, and was written explicitly into the locked canon rule governing recruitment.

The lesson generalizes past this one document: age plus sexualized-content combinations in fiction are a hard stop, not a judgment call to make silently or route around by paraphrasing. Quote the exact problem, state the refusal plainly, and wait for a real correction before any further work on that material, even under an otherwise-granted "full creative autonomy."
