## Body

The cult-network execution plan drafted earlier this project (populating T.D.K.'s cult network across the Shattered Kingdoms) is now fully executed. All five source dossiers are extracted into the ledger: the Weighing Communities/Proximity Faithful, the Concordat of the Long Account, the Pavilion of the True Weight, the Still-Water Compact, and the Null-Walkers. All three cults the plan flagged as needing full expansion from scratch (no source document existed) are also built and locked: the Slab Compact, the Null Caucus, and the Frequency Vigil.

`canon-ledger.json`, the project's canon-rules ledger (a Claude Project doc, not a file inside this Core), sits at ledger_version 2.4, 510 total rules, 21 batches completed, zero duplicate rule IDs as of 2026-08-23. Fourteen distinct cult organizations now exist in the ledger under the `CULT-` prefix.

What remains open in this project, not urgent: the standalone Domus Inviolate Dossier still needs its own deep-read; the newly-added Ashkeel institution (batch 19, renamed from an uploaded "Guild of the Extraordinary" document) has only its founding/governance layer built, with the Seven High Arts, the collar/legal hierarchy, internal geography and guilds, and named characters all still queued; the 173-file Character Codex zip and the World Adaptation Blueprint remain untouched; and three long-standing open items (OPEN-005, OPEN-007, OPEN-008) are still unresolved.

**Two corrections:**

1. **"Fourteen distinct cult organizations" appears to undercount.** Summing the organizations named in
   the batch records gives **17**: batches 12, 13 and 14 carry three each (9), batch 15 three, batch 16
   two, and batches 18, 20 and 21 one each. Ashkeel (batch 19) is an institution rather than a cult and
   is excluded from that count. Worth a recount before the figure is quoted anywhere load-bearing.
2. **"The 173-file Character Codex zip remains untouched" is ambiguous.** Batch 10 in the ledger *is*
   `LORDS_OF_CIAN_Character_Codex_definitive.docx`, with 80 rules already drafted at
   `draft_pending_operator_discussion`. The untouched item is the separate
   `All_Psychological_Profiles.zip` in the Lore Vault. These are two different artifacts and the note
   conflates them.
